Our promise to you

This is your diary, and it is private. MoodCast is a diary, not a medical service, and it takes no part in diagnosis or treatment. The AI reads your writing only to help you see your own state of mind.

Your diary is read on this device and never leaves it. Your weekly mood light is calculated on this device and is never sent anywhere. If you turn on the weekly forecast, only a short digest that you review goes to Gemini, and the report comes back to you alone.

Because of this, no one else will know when you are struggling. MoodCast will not call a helpline, contact anyone, or report anything on your behalf. When you notice that something is wrong, a friend, a counselor or a doctor can help. If you'd rather not talk to someone you know, you can look up support services where you live; in the US, you can call or text 988 any time.

MoodCast is not surveillance software.

AI may read your private writing, but reading is not ownership. It gives no right to further process, monitor or send your data.

The light is computed on this device with simple rules. It describes mood only; it is not a clinical measure and never detects any condition.

How the light works

Green: neither rule below applies. Yellow: 3 or more yellow or red days. Red: 3 or more red days, or any day that mentions hurting yourself or not wanting to live.

Weekly mood forecast

The forecast is off. When you turn it on:

  • Only this week's digest is sent, never your diary. You will see every line before it goes, and can edit or remove any of it.
  • We don't ask for your name or an account.
  • Like any internet request, it goes to Google's servers along with standard technical data such as your IP address.
  • The forecast can help you notice patterns you may have missed.

Review the digest. Edit or delete any line. Nothing is sent until you press the button.

About MoodCast

MoodCast is a diary with a mirror. It reflects your own state back to you, like a personal mood check-in. It is not a therapist, a crisis service or a monitoring system, and it is not a medical device.
